RESIDENT BASIN GENERATION AND SURFACE INSPECTION

Open the installed Mac app's terrain inspection mode:
  open -n ~/particleengine/out/macos-release/ParticleWorld.app --args --terrain-preview
Or run its executable with --terrain-preview --frames 180 for a bounded smoke run.
The normal launch still opens the particle sandbox. Inspection now includes
optional GPU collision probes (see GPU_Voxel_Motion.txt), without grain-to-grain
contacts or editing commands. Water, burning, destruction and deposition remain
open. Escape closes it.

The version 1 deterministic recipe uses 256 chunks across [-16,16) x [0,16) x
[-16,16) metres. Each chunk has 32 cubed voxels with .125 m spacing. It produces
stone flooring and a two-voxel-thick rim, a four-metre stone ledge, a sand slope,
a shallow dry pool recess, and a 2 x 2 m wood patch. Integer hashing varies the
top layer of the sand slope by seed. There is no floating-point random generator
or GPU-dependent scene generation. Water and thermal state will be introduced
with their solvers rather than represented as solid occupancy now.

The generator validates distinct material IDs, creates unique voxel edits, and
commits one CPU batch into a fresh store. Seed 1337 contains 404,546 occupied
voxels and 170,188 exposed faces. CPU tests compare every voxel in all 256 chunks
for repeatability, check named feature locations, and verify that seed changes
affect sand occupancy. Seeds 1337 and 42 differ at 1,566 voxel locations.

VoxelTerrain builds an immutable mesh from the exposed-face extractor. Hidden
faces across chunk boundaries are omitted. Vertices are origin-relative positions
and packed material color with directional face lighting; indices are uint32.
The payload is 88 bytes per face: four 16-byte vertices and six uint32 indices.
A 1,048,576-face guard prevents an unbounded mesh allocation. No greedy merging,
chunk culling, dirty-mesh reuse or LOD is implemented yet. All occupied materials
must be solid. The full resident occupancy remains independent of camera view.

Both constructor and draw require the exact source store, shared origin and
current GpuVoxelWorld revision. A stale mesh cannot draw alongside newer occupancy.
Inspection rebuilds stage a fresh store, occupancy snapshot and mesh before
replacing the prior publication together on the main thread. Failure leaves the
prior publication intact. This is a static render publication, not a terrain /
particle edit transaction or GPU acknowledgement. Mesh and occupancy GPU payload
for seed 1337 is about 46.3 MiB, excluding allocator overhead and temporary CPU /
upload buffers. Rebuilds temporarily hold old and new publications concurrently.

Material hot reload is not enabled in inspection mode.
The panel offers orbit, distance, height and a nonnegative integer seed. Rebuild
regenerates the basin and clears diagnostic probes. Drop collision probes starts
256 grains under gravity against occupancy; Pause probes pauses them. Material
colors come from the existing material library.
Native UI inspection confirmed the seed 1337 view and a successful seed 42 rebuild
(404,578 voxels / 170,228 faces). GPU image tests read rendered pixels for all six
faces, including negative coordinates, depth ordering, solid material color,
empty rebuilds and stale publication rejection. A chunk-seam fixture checks that
two adjacent voxels produce ten exposed faces.

Remaining integration: connect terrain response to the full granular predictor,
pair position correction, velocity finishing and settling; the full basin simulation grid; consistent
terrain edits and physical debris/deposition transactions; dirty chunk mesh reuse;
origin rebases at tick boundaries, fluids, thermal state and persistence.
