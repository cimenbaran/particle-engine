SETTLED-GRAIN DEPOSITION REFERENCE — macOS preparation

planDeposits is a bounded, read-only CPU reference for the future GPU claim pass.
It accepts one immutable particle/history/terrain epoch and returns proposed
one-voxel claims with two generation-qualified grain owners and explicit mass
balances. It never edits occupancy, retires particles, advances a ledger, or
reads the GPU. It is not connected to the live simulation.

Input limits are 4096 particle slots, 256 materials and 512 output voxels per
call (default quota 32). Eligible owners are live solid particles with common
.0625 m radius and the exact density-derived encoded inverse mass. Their default
settling history must match generation, have 30 quiet ticks, candidate=1 and no
unsupported grace ticks. Current speed must be at most the .1 m/s hold limit.
Other solid particles, including static or ineligible grains, remain blockers.
Malformed live identity or solid numeric state rejects the reference input.

Claims are considered in ascending stable-slot order. The first owner's current
cell is the target, inside the zero-origin resident basin. The cell must be
resident and empty, and sit on y=0 or above existing solid terrain. Claims do not
use other proposals as support; later acknowledged ticks can grow another layer.
The second owner has the same material and minimizes squared distance to the
cell centre, with stable-slot tie breaking. Its centre must be within .1875 m on
every axis. Previously rejected owners may become partners of a later target.

Each pair must pass planGrainDeposit with the requested tolerance, capped at 5%.
For this common-radius profile, two grains produce one voxel with approximately
4.5% signed mass loss; one grain cannot fund a voxel within tolerance. Values use
actual encoded inverse mass and are retained per claim/material. Different
materials cannot cancel each other's errors. Strict tolerances can reject all
pairs. This is quantized conversion, not exact mass conservation.

No grain or voxel may be claimed twice. A proposed voxel must not strictly
intersect any remaining solid sphere; exact tangency is allowed. Previously
accepted owners are omitted because the eventual joint transaction would retire
them together. A blocked nearest pair is skipped; the reference does not search
all partner combinations. Other owners may propose a different valid target.
Failed or quota-deferred claims leave all original data intact.

Remaining integration
GPU candidate generation/arbitration and clearance must be checked against this
reference. Production must freeze the source epoch, stage candidate occupancy,
validate terrain and particle acknowledgements, then publish occupancy before
retiring exactly acknowledged generation-qualified owners. Failure must retain
dynamic grains. Deposit mass ledgers, wake handling, thermal transfer and live
controls remain open. CPU reference tests do not prove any of those GPU gates.
