PRIVATE GPU DEPOSITION CANDIDATES — macOS / Metal

GpuDepositCandidates is the first GPU stage of deposition. It reads immutable
particle state, settling history and a current zero-origin resident occupancy
snapshot. It owns a density-derived mass profile table and separate output and
status buffers. It cannot write terrain, particle state or history. Capacity is
0..4096 slots, material count 1..256, and tolerance 0..5%.

The host precomputes each material's exact encoded inverse mass and whether two
grains can fund one voxel at that tolerance. This avoids a second, differently
rounded GPU mass calculation. The source epoch must match those profiles; a
future coordinator must freeze/validate the owner, material encoding and tick.
No production full-particle readback is introduced.

One parallel dispatch produces two uvec4 lanes per slot:
- signed voxel cell x/y/z, material ID;
- stable slot, generation, kind (0 ignored, 1 solid blocker, 2 eligible owner),
  and a flag for a supported empty target at that cell.
Eligible partners may have an unavailable target of their own. Repeated cells
are intentional at this stage; these are candidates, not exclusive claims.

Live identities require flags=1, matching slot, nonzero generation and a known
material. Nonsolids and dead slots are ignored. Solid numeric state is checked;
nonfinite values, invalid radius or negative inverse mass reject the operation.
Positive subnormal radii also reject because Metal float clearance cannot safely
handle them. Signed subnormal positions are preserved when forming grid cells,
including negative values immediately below zero.

Owners require the common .0625 m radius, exact inverse-mass bits, admissible
pair mass, matching default settling generation, 30 quiet ticks, candidate=1 and
no unsupported grace. A rounded-up squared-speed bound checks the .1 m/s hold
limit. It conservatively defers a narrow floating-point band near the limit,
including exact .1 m/s. Settling grains well below the threshold are unaffected;
it cannot admit an above-limit grain through dot-product rounding. This is a
specified conservative difference from the double-precision CPU reference.

A ready target must be inside the basin and resident, empty, and either at y=0
or above existing solid terrain. Occupied cells, missing residency, unsupported
cells and nonsolid support do not become ready. Occupancy is never changed.
The four status words contain error bits (1 identity, 2 numeric), eligible-owner
count, ready-target count and valid-solid count. Any error invalidates the entire
candidate set; counts alone cannot authorize a partial deposit.

The pass validates views, storage aliases and source/origin/revision identity.
Particle/history storage must cover capacity records of 64/16 bytes respectively.
Inputs must remain alive and unchanged until execution completes. Re-submission
overwrites only private output/status. It uses at most six storage bindings.

Remaining work: deterministic partner/target arbitration, remaining-sphere
clearance, compact claim acknowledgement, candidate terrain publication and
acknowledged retirement. This stage is not connected to live deposition.
