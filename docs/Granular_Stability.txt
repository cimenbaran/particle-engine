GRANULAR STABILITY FIXTURES

Run the installed executable with --granular-check --report result.json.
On macOS:
  out/macos-release/ParticleWorld.app/Contents/MacOS/ParticleWorld --granular-check --report out/granular-stability.json
On Windows, add --software-gpu for D3D11 WARP validation. The CI matrix runs
this mode at both 128 and 256 threads and uploads the report and console log.
Exit code 0 means all fixtures passed; 1 indicates failure. Setup/startup
failures may exit before a JSON report exists; retain the console log as well.

The fixed profile uses radius 0.0625 m, inverse mass 1, restitution 0,
friction 0.5, four substeps, four Jacobi iterations and a 12 m/s speed cap.
Five fixtures run 600 ticks each: a falling column of 16, a resting cube of
64, a cube against a wall, 64 coincident spawns, and 64 particles in a closed
zero-gravity dissipative fixture. There are also 129 high-speed wall shots
(30 ticks) and a grazing floor particle (60 ticks). Total: 3,090 ticks.
The initial arrays and deterministic seeded velocities are recorded in JSON.

At tick 1, every 30 ticks and the final tick, readback checks finite state,
immutable input bytes, persistent identity/material/mass, lifetime arithmetic,
inset enclosure containment and the speed cap. Solver faults latch every tick.
Age is checked against repeated float substeps; drift from real-valued elapsed
time is reported separately. Fixtures do not exercise pool allocation; the
separate --gpu-test app-path audit covers membership and allocation invariants.

For each settling fixture, every sampled state in the final two seconds must
have at most 1% of contacts penetrating more than 10% of radius and maximum
speed at most 0.1 m/s. Contacts use centre distance <= 0.1251 m. The report also
records gravitational potential and mechanical energy. A cube can collapse
from a temporary symmetric arrangement, lowering gravitational energy; an
early snapshot alone is not sufficient evidence of settling.

The two closed-energy fixtures must never exceed initial kinetic energy at a
sample and must finish no higher than their halfway sample, within 1e-4 times
max(1, initial energy). This is sampled evidence, not an every-tick energy proof.
Coincident initial overlaps can inject projection energy and are assessed for
bounded speed and eventual settling, not conservation of initial energy.

These small fixtures establish reproducible regression coverage. They do not
certify arbitrary scene stability, physical D3D11 hardware, interior terrain,
performance, or ten-minute soaks. Elapsed time includes readbacks and setup.
See tests/results/m3-max-granular-stability.txt for local Metal measurements.
