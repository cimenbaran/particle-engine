GRANULAR STABILITY FIXTURES

Run the installed executable with --granular-check --report result.json.
On macOS:
  out/macos-release/ParticleWorld.app/Contents/MacOS/ParticleWorld --granular-check --report out/granular-stability.json
On Windows, add --software-gpu for D3D11 WARP validation. The CI matrix runs
this mode at both 128 and 256 threads and uploads the report and console log.
Exit code 0 means all fixtures passed; 1 indicates failure. Setup/startup
failures may exit before a JSON report exists; retain the console log as well.

The fixed profile uses radius 0.0625 m, inverse mass 1, restitution 0,
friction 0.5, four substeps, four Jacobi iterations and a 12 m/s speed cap.
Five fixtures run 600 ticks each: a falling column of 16, a resting cube of
64, a cube against a wall, 64 coincident spawns, and 64 particles in a closed
zero-gravity dissipative fixture. There are also 129 high-speed wall shots
(30 ticks) and a grazing floor particle (60 ticks). The sphere extension adds
64 grains falling onto a radius-1.25 sphere (600 ticks), a four-body stack on
the same sphere (600 ticks), and nine 12 m/s shots at a radius-0.9375 sphere
with zero gravity (240 ticks). Total: ten scenes and 4,530 ticks. The original
seven fixtures and thresholds are unchanged. Workload: granular_stability_sphere_v2.
The initial arrays and deterministic seeded velocities are recorded in JSON.

At tick 1, every 30 ticks and the final tick, readback checks finite state,
immutable input bytes, persistent identity/material/mass, lifetime arithmetic,
inset enclosure containment and the speed cap. Solver faults latch every tick.
Age is checked against repeated float substeps; drift from real-valued elapsed
time is reported separately. Fixtures do not exercise pool allocation; the
separate --gpu-test app-path audit covers membership and allocation invariants.

For each settling fixture, every sampled state in the final two seconds must
have at most 1% of contacts penetrating more than 10% of radius and maximum
speed at most 0.1 m/s. Contacts use centre distance <= 0.1251 m. The report also
records gravitational potential and mechanical energy. A cube can collapse
from a temporary symmetric arrangement, lowering gravitational energy; an
early snapshot alone is not sufficient evidence of settling.

The three closed-energy fixtures must never exceed initial kinetic energy at a
sample and must finish no higher than their halfway sample, within 1e-4 times
max(1, initial energy). This is sampled evidence, not an every-tick energy proof.
Coincident initial overlaps can inject projection energy and are assessed for
bounded speed and eventual settling, not conservation of initial energy.

These small fixtures establish reproducible regression coverage. They do not
certify arbitrary scene stability, physical D3D11 hardware, voxel terrain,
performance, or ten-minute soaks. Elapsed time includes readbacks and setup.
See tests/results/m3-max-granular-stability.txt for local Metal measurements.

Per-particle diagnostic snapshots
Each verified sample now includes particle_state: persistent slot/generation,
position, velocity, radius and inverse mass for every fixture particle. These
come from the existing readback; no extra GPU passes, samples or gate changes
are introduced. fastest_particle selects the lowest slot on an exact speed tie
and records its speed, six signed wall gaps and nearby particle geometry.
Wall-gap order is x-min, x-max, y-min, y-max, z-min, z-max. Negative surface gap
means penetration. Nearby records are within 0.002 m surface separation. Their
unit normals point from the other body toward the fastest body; negative relative
normal speed means approach. Coincident centres have null normal/speed components,
not a fabricated geometric normal. These are geometric observations after a tick,
not the actual sequence of normals or impulses used during its solver iterations.

Snapshot fields are diagnostic additions to schema 1. They are not a recording
format and cannot yet be passed to the replay CLI. Existing initial_state and
invariant metadata remain authoritative for the fixed workload. This information
supports comparing backend trajectories and identifying the body responsible for
a sampled settling violation, rather than inferring a cause from maximum speed.

Sphere geometry gate
Each scene records its sphere centre/radius (zero radius disables it). Each
sample records minimum_sphere_gap, computed in double precision across all
particles against sphere radius plus 0.0625 m. Negative values denote overlap;
values below -1e-6 m fail immediately. This small allowance addresses float
geometry evaluation and is independent of the existing particle-pair overlap
gate. Disabled spheres report null. Sphere drop/stack must also pass the same
final-120-tick settling window; sphere impacts must pass the existing closed
energy check. No threshold is relaxed for terrain.

Current Metal result: the sphere drop fails the settling speed window at tick570
(0.239068 m/s). Both workgroups reproduce it; sphere containment, stack and
impact-energy cases pass. See tests/results/m3-max-sphere-stability.txt.
