SPHERE TERRAIN SCENE

Run ParticleWorld --scene assets/scenes/sphere_drop.json --capacity 256,
or choose Scene presets -> Sand over sphere -> Load preset in the app.
The scene enables granular physics automatically. It contains 256 sand particles
above a fixed sphere at (0,2,0), radius 1.25 m. Empty/reset preserve the obstacle.
Load a scene without terrain before disabling particle collisions. Loading a
non-terrain preset removes the collider and its renderer while keeping granular
mode enabled until explicitly switched off.

Schema version 1 remains unchanged. Version 2 adds exactly one sphere object,
with centre (three finite numbers) and positive radius. AnalyticSphere validation
requires radius 0.0625..4 and separation from enclosure planes. Initial spawn
bounds must be disjoint from the sphere expanded by the common particle radius
0.0625 m, and fit the granular enclosure. Intersecting spawn brushes are rejected
with a visible message; this is a conservative whole-box rejection, not partial
sampling. Existing ballistic recordings reject sphere scenes until the granular
recording profile is implemented.

The solver, settling tracker and renderer consume the same scene geometry. The
sphere is fixed terrain, has no particle slot or inverse mass, and does not alter
active/free pool ownership. Swept collision, position correction, final velocity
constraints and upward settling support are handled by their existing GPU stages.
The render surface is an opaque, depth-tested 48-by-96 tessellated sphere with
smooth lighting. Its polygonal surface approximates the analytic collision shape;
particle billboards retain their existing centre-depth rendering limitation.
Mesh payload: 49*97*3*4 + 48*96*6*2 = 112332 bytes, reported separately as
terrain_geometry. Shader/uniform overhead remains outside requested payload totals.

Validation includes strict schema/round-trip/spawn checks and an application GPU
fixture that rests a particle on the sphere for 60 ticks, acquires a settling
candidate, rejects intersecting spawns and unsupported ballistic switching, then
loads an empty preset and removes terrain resources. Native Metal UI validation
loaded the preset both through CLI and the menu, observed 256 active particles and
256 settling candidates at paused tick 1412 without a displayed solver fault,
and reloaded the preset to tick zero. This is not a stability/performance gate.
