SETTLING CANDIDATE CONTRACT

src/core/Settling.hpp implements and tests the sidecar state
transition; tests/reference/SettlingSupport.hpp is an independent all-pairs
support oracle. The GPU tracker is implemented and validated against these references;
see GPU_Settling.txt. Interactive integration retains candidates as active matter.

One 16-byte history belongs to one physical slot: generation, consecutive quiet
ticks, consecutive unsupported ticks and candidate (0/1). History never changes
particle alive flags, inverse mass, velocity, allocation or rendering. Candidates
remain active matter. They are not sleeping particles or acknowledged deposition.
The owner must clear all histories on scene/mode reset, including when generations
are reused, and advance exactly once per completed fixed tick, not per substep
or render frame. A recycled generation starts fresh. Failed ticks, nonfinite or
negative speed, zero generation, and ineligible bodies invalidate history.

Default acquisition requires 30 consecutive ticks at speed <=0.05 m/s with tight
support contact. An interruption resets acquisition. A candidate survives speed
up to 0.1 m/s and a looser contact test. Speed >0.1 releases immediately. Three
consecutive unsupported ticks release; recovery resets the missing-contact count.
All thresholds are inclusive except the release speed comparison. Counters are
bounded; even UINT32_MAX dwell/grace profiles cannot wrap. Profiles must have
finite nonnegative entry speed, a strictly greater exit speed, and positive tick
counts. Replacing a profile requires an owner reset; structurally invalid history
is also discarded defensively. Invalid contact flags (entry without retention)
are a caller error and throw rather than silently admitting inconsistent evidence.

Initial geometry contract: common radius 0.0625 m, floor at y=0, gravity down Y.
Eligible candidates are live dynamic solids. Support may be another live solid,
including a static one. Sphere contact normals must point upward with Y >=0.5;
side/upper contacts and coincident centres do not qualify. Tight support allows
surface gap <=0.0001 m; retention permits <=0.002 m. Penetration deeper than
0.00625 m (10% of radius) disqualifies that contact for either test. Floor support
uses the same gap/penetration limits. Nonfinite geometry cannot supply support.
This is local candidate evidence, not proof that the support chain reaches ground.

Validation covers exact dwell boundaries, speed/contact dead bands, interrupted
acquisition, support-loss grace and recovery, impulses, recycling, deletion/phase
ineligibility, failed ticks, nonfinite inputs, malformed history, one-tick and
UINT32_MAX profiles, 100,000 steady ticks, floor/stack/static support, side contacts,
coincident centres and deep overlap. CTest name: settling_hysteresis.

The live tracker consumes final committed GPU state and exposes delayed counts;
an application fixture verifies physical active-list membership. Do not
use snapshot-only diagnostics to accumulate dwell: they miss intervening ticks.
