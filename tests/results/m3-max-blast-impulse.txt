235 impulse batches inspect 1,553,038 particle slots per profile:
- capacities 0,1,127,128,129,255,256,257,1024,4096,65537 and 1048576;
- a uniform fixture accepts exactly the first 12 affordable slots from an 8 J
  budget, including arrays large enough to overflow a wrapping cost sum;
- zero/tiny budgets preserve every velocity; a large budget admits all recipients;
- zero requested speed is a no-op;
- 208 random batches cover every pair of 16 energy magnitudes and 13 speeds,
  varied inverse mass, pre-existing motion and dead/static/nonsolid exclusions;
- explicit centre, near-centre, exact and adjacent radius boundaries, braking,
  capped speed, mass endpoints and maximum slot generation;
- malformed ownership/generation/material/numeric inputs reject the whole batch,
  preserve private output as a copy of the source, and charge zero;
- invalid host input, aliases and repeat submission reject.

An independent double-precision radial oracle checks accepted velocities. Actual
positive kinetic-energy changes are summed without crediting braking and must
not exceed the returned ticket charge or supplied energy. Non-velocity fields
and the original source are checked byte-for-byte. Outputs stay within 12 m/s.
Production uses private GPU buffers; full-state readbacks are test-only.

The scan fixture now tests both wrapping and saturating arithmetic at all 15
sizes and four patterns, comparing every prefix, total and unchanged input to
independent CPU sums through 1,048,576 elements. Existing radix/physics/rendering
fixtures retain the default wrapping variant and pass in the complete suite.

Initial loading correctly rejected the new scan variant until its exact source
name and PW_SCAN_SATURATE=1 definition were added to shader-manifest validation.
Manifest validation remains enabled for both new variants.

This verifies a private impulse primitive, not live adoption. The remaining work
