GPU BLAST DUST / SPARKS — APPLE M3 MAX / METAL

AppleClang 21.0.0.21000099. Complete --gpu-test and all 26 native CTests pass
for workgroups 128 and 256. Default 128 is restored for installation.

Nine real Metal scenarios verify:
- family capacities (dust/spark): 0/1, 1/0, 1/1, 127/129, 128/256, 512/256,
  2048/2048; a disabled/full family cannot consume the other's capacity;
- exact CPU seed upload, slot identity/generation, zero inverse mass and lifetime;
- every live trajectory against an independent double-precision one-step oracle;
- fading render-instance alpha, complete expiration after the maximum lifetime,
  retained drop counts, generation reuse and reset of all state/counters;
- full-pool overflow leaves every existing record byte-identical;
- generation UINT32_MAX retires permanently rather than wrapping;
- invalid capacities, views, timestep and quotas reject;
- a nonzero physical command pool remains byte-identical through cosmetic work;
- a real offscreen render is visible initially, has approximately half RGB energy
  at half lifetime and is completely black after expiration.

The initial shared physical fragment shader forced alpha=1, so the cosmetic path
now uses fs_cosmetics with lifetime alpha and soft disk coverage. The offscreen
pixel check verifies the final fragment result, not only instance buffer values.

The preview is wired to consume one accepted-blast event, emit 48 dust/16 sparks,
advance an independent paused-aware fixed clock, show counts, and clear cosmetics
on reset/rebuild/mode changes. Effects never feed physical physics, mass or heat.
The existing eleven staged-command tests retain one-shot acknowledgement gates.
