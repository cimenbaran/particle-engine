The new reservation fixture covers 360 cases and 778,788 slot checks per
configuration, for capacities 0,1,127,128,129,255,256,257,4096,16384.

Each case checks the complete private particle snapshot and ordered claim
records against a CPU allocator, the 16-byte GPU status, and unchanged input
particle bytes. Sources include empty, mixed, full, generation-exhausted and
invalid identities. Requests include exact fit, zero, one past available,
partial use and UINT_MAX. Reusing one allocator across successful and failed
requests verifies that stale claims do not survive. Host aliases reject.
Generation zero increments to one; UINT_MAX-1 reserves the last generation;
UINT_MAX is excluded without wrapping. Invalid alive slot IDs, zero-generation
live particles and already-reserved flags reject the whole batch.

The reservation is isolated from live state and terrain. It is not yet a
coordinated physical conversion. Debris initialization/clearance, base-tick
freshness, mass accounting and joint publication remain required. No normal
ParticlePool may consume the private reserved snapshot before activation.
