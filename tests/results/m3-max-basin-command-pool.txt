tracing identified position correction, source bit 64 (collision-event budget),
not motion prediction. Correction now gathers all nearby contact normals when
revisiting a retained voxel, without changing its 8-normal/16-event limits.
Temporary trace APIs, buffers and shader writes were removed before validation.
